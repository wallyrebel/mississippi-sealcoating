Why Sealcoating?

The Science Behind Asphalt Protection

UV Protection

Mississippi's intense sun breaks down asphalt binder, causing oxidation, fading, and brittleness. Sealcoating blocks harmful UV rays, keeping your asphalt flexible and resilient.

Water Resistance

North Mississippi gets well over 50 inches of rain a year. Water seeps into unsealed cracks, eroding the base and causing structural failure. Sealcoating helps shed water before it gets in.

Chemical Resistance

Oil, gasoline, and automotive fluids dissolve asphalt binder on contact. Sealcoating acts as a chemical shield, preventing these substances from softening your pavement.

Cost Savings

Sealcoating costs pennies per square foot compared to dollars for repaving. Regular maintenance saves property owners a large share of lifetime pavement costs.

Curb Appeal

Nothing looks better than a freshly sealed, jet-black asphalt surface. Sealcoating instantly enhances the appearance of any property, residential or commercial.

Longevity

With regular sealcoating every 2–3 years and prompt crack repair, your asphalt driveway or parking lot can last far longer than unmaintained pavement.

What areas of North Mississippi do you serve?

We're based in Ripley and serve 19 North Mississippi counties, including Tippah, Alcorn, Prentiss, Pontotoc, Lafayette, Lee and DeSoto counties, and cities like Corinth, Booneville, Oxford, Tupelo and Southaven.

How often should an asphalt driveway or parking lot be sealcoated?

Most driveways and parking lots should be sealcoated every two to three years, with cracks sealed as soon as they appear. New asphalt should cure for about 6–12 months before its first sealcoat. Learn more about sealcoating.

Do you repair cracks and potholes before sealcoating?

Yes. We seal cracks with hot-pour rubberized sealant and patch potholes before sealcoating so the finished surface lasts. See our asphalt repair services.
